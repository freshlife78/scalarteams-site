Practical Case · Logistics Supply Chain & 3PL

Automated freight quote & bill-of-lading triage in high-volume 3PL inboxes

Large freight brokers (including C.H. Robinson’s deployment of LLM inbox agents) parse unstructured shipper emails, PDF rate confirmations, and bills of lading directly into TMS fields—cutting manual quote-to-booking keying from minutes to seconds while routing exceptions to human coordinators.

Engineering Practice Architecture

Workflows vs. Autonomous Agents: why simple composable patterns win in production

Anthropic’s engineering team published their findings from dozens of production deployments: teams that succeed avoid heavy, opaque agent frameworks and instead build deterministic code workflows using five composable patterns—prompt chaining, routing, parallelization, orchestrator-workers, and evaluator-optimizers.

CTO takeaway: When vetting senior AI engineers, test whether they reach for clean control loops and deterministic tool schemas before spinning up unconstrained multi-agent loops.

Vision-LLM extraction for multi-page vendor invoices and three-way PO matching

Distribution and wholesale finance teams are pairing multimodal document models with NetSuite and QuickBooks to extract line-item SKUs, unit prices, and freight surcharges from non-standard vendor PDFs, automatically flagging price or quantity variances against warehouse receiving logs.

Controller takeaway: Automated OCR+LLM extraction handles the first pass of voucher creation, while your AP specialist resolves the 15–20% of invoices with landed-cost discrepancies, short shipments, or damaged goods.

Engineering Practice RAG & Search

Contextual Retrieval + BM25 + Reranking cuts RAG retrieval failures by 67%

Naive vector chunking strips crucial context from financial tables, contracts, and technical docs (e.g., losing which vendor or quarter a paragraph refers to). Prepending chunk-specific context at indexing time and combining dense embeddings with BM25 lexical search and a cross-encoder reranker dramatically improves accuracy.

CTO takeaway: This is a core production test we run with AI engineering candidates—diagnosing retrieval misses when user queries include exact SKU codes, part numbers, or legal clauses.
Source: Anthropic Research & Engineering Read benchmark →
Model Capability Production APIs

Constrained decoding and 100% JSON-Schema compliance in frontier models

Modern frontier APIs from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google now enforce strict context-free grammars during token sampling, guaranteeing that model outputs match Pydantic or Zod schemas without malformed JSON, missing required keys, or hallucinated enum values.

Engineering takeaway: Enables reliable integration between probabilistic LLMs and strict ERP/SQL backends—essential for automated ledger coding, customs tariff classification, and tool calling.

Model Releases Cost & Latency Routing

Two-tier model routing: pairing reasoning models with fast small models

Across the latest Claude, GPT, Gemini, and open-weight Qwen/Llama releases, production architectures have converged on model routing: using reasoning models for complex multi-step planning, tax/contract edge cases, and synthetic data generation, while routing 85%+ of high-throughput classification to distilled small models.

Engineering takeaway: Model routing and prompt caching routinely cut production inference bills by 60–80% while keeping sub-second UI responsiveness.
