Frequently Asked Questions

Questions CTOs and VPs of Engineering ask

How do you prevent proxy interviewers, AI interview assistants, and fake identities?

Every candidate undergoes live video identity verification against government-issued photo ID, employment history and manager reference checks, and a live technical session where requirements are modified mid-walkthrough so off-screen prompting or proxy helpers cannot pass.

Do I have to book a sales call to get pricing?

No. Submit your role requirements and stack through our quote form and we will send a complete written quote by email. We only get on a call if and when you ask for one.

How do you separate engineers who have shipped production LLM systems from people who have only built notebook demos?

We test candidates on retrieval quality, evaluation harnesses, latency/cost trade-offs, structured outputs, failure handling, and observability—then require a live walkthrough of code they wrote and defended under changing requirements.

How much time-zone overlap do your engineers have with US Pacific and Eastern teams?

All engineers are based in Latin America or Canada and work with at least 6 hours of daily overlap with US Pacific Time (and full workday alignment with Mountain, Central, and Eastern Time).

How are engineers employed and billed?

Engineers are hired and employed through our office in Ecuador, where Scalar Teams acts as employer of record. They work full-time and exclusively for your team, and we bill monthly in advance in USD.

What engineering roles do you place besides Senior AI / LLM Engineers?

Alongside Senior AI / LLM Engineers, we place Machine Learning Engineers, Full-Stack AI Product Engineers (Python/TypeScript/React), and Data Engineers who build the pipelines and warehouses supporting AI applications.
