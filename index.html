Questions owners ask

What happens in a flow audit?

Two or three working sessions by video with you and the people who do the work. We map each process step by step, time the manual parts and leave you with maps and a written plan you keep, whether or not you hire us.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. You know how your business runs. We work out which parts software can do reliably and which should stay with your people.

What size of business is this for?

Owner-led companies with roughly 10 to 200 people, where a few staff spend hours a day moving information between emails, spreadsheets and systems.

Which systems do you work with?

QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, Shopify, common inventory and dispatch tools, email and spreadsheets. If something can’t be connected, the audit tells you before you spend more.

Who owns what gets built?

You do. It runs in your accounts and is documented, so it stays with you if you stop working with us.

Who employs the engineer?

Our team in Ecuador is the employer and handles payroll, benefits and equipment. You set priorities and direct the day-to-day work.

What if it doesn’t work out?

We replace the engineer at no cost. To end the engagement, give one week’s notice in the first 60 days and 30 days after that.

Can we hire the engineer directly later?

Yes. The buyout is 3× the monthly fee in months 1–6, 1.5× in months 7–12, and free after a year.

Do I have to get on a sales call?

Not to get a price. Scope and fee arrive in writing. The audit itself is working time with you and your team.

Do you work with Canadian companies?

Yes. We’re based in British Columbia and bill in USD or CAD, monthly in advance.

Can I just hire an AI engineer?

Yes. We still place senior AI, machine learning and data engineers on the same terms. Say so in the form.

You’re new. Why trust you?

Because you only pay for the audit up front, you keep the plan, and every later step is month to month. We earn more only if the first step was worth it.
