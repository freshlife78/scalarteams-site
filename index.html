How It Works

From role brief to full-time team member in three steps

Every placement is full-time and dedicated to your company. We handle recruitment, vetting, cross-border contracts, and local employment through our Ecuador office.

Step 01

Tell us the role

Send your requirements, systems, time-zone hours, and target start date using our quote form. No discovery call is required—we reply in writing with a quote and role specification.

Step 02

Review a written shortlist

Receive a shortlist of identity-verified candidates who have passed our technical, workflow, and English evaluations. Every profile includes our written assessment notes so you only interview candidates who fit.

Step 03

Hire and we handle employment

Select your candidate and direct their daily work just like any in-house team member. We employ them directly through our Ecuador office as employer of record, handle local payroll and compliance, and send one monthly invoice in advance in USD.
