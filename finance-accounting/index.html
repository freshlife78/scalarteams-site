Why Controllers Call Us

When transaction volume grows faster than your accounting headcount

In distribution, importing, and 3PL operations, accounting is tied to physical movement: purchase orders, bills of lading, warehouse receipts, Lumper fees, and customer deductions. When your team is short-staffed, the backlog compounds fast.

“Our AP clerk quit and vendor bills are piling up.”

Inventory receipts, carrier freight bills, and customs brokerage invoices need three-way matching every day. One departure leaves your controller keying invoices at night just to keep vendors off credit hold.

“Month-end close keeps slipping past day 15.”

Accruals for in-transit inventory, unbilled freight, bank reconciliations, and intercompany clearing get pushed back because your senior accountants are buried in daily transaction entry.

“Shipment volume is up, but we don’t have domestic headcount budget.”

Your sales and warehouse teams are moving more pallets and containers, creating more AR invoicing, short-pay disputes, and collections follow-up—without budget for another local hire.

Controls & Security

Designed around a controller’s internal controls

Adding nearshore finance staff should strengthen your control environment, not weaken it. We structure every placement around segregation of duties.

Least-privilege system access

Your team member works inside your existing IT environment, VPN, or remote desktop with role-based permissions scoped only to the modules they need in QuickBooks, NetSuite, or your TMS/WMS.

No unsupervised bank authority

Staff prepare vouchers, reconcile statements, and stage payment batches, but never hold unilateral authority to release wires, sign checks, or add unverified vendor bank instructions without your internal sign-off.

Verified manager references & direct employment

Before presenting a shortlist, we conduct direct reference checks with prior supervisors. Once hired, the professional is employed directly through our Ecuador office under formal confidentiality agreements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions controllers and CFOs ask

No. Our staff work within your existing internal controls under least-privilege permissions. They prepare bills, reconcile statements, and stage payment runs inside your ERP or accounting software, while final payment release and bank authority stay strictly with your internal approvers.

How do you verify candidates actually know US GAAP, QuickBooks, or NetSuite?

Every candidate completes a practical accounting and workflow assessment overseen by our founder, a CPA with 25 years in wholesale distribution and freight. We test accrual accounting, three-way PO matching, freight and landed-cost allocation, bank reconciliations, and ERP navigation before you review their profile.

Who employs the accountant or coordinator, and how does billing work?

Scalar Teams hires and employs the talent directly through our office in Ecuador, acting as employer of record. We handle local employment contracts, payroll, and statutory compliance, and bill your company monthly in advance in USD.

What time zone do your finance and logistics staff work?

Ecuador is on Eastern Standard Time (UTC-5) year-round, and our Latin American and Canadian talent work your business hours across Eastern, Central, Mountain, or Pacific time.

Are placements part-time or shared across multiple clients?

Neither. Every placement is full-time and dedicated exclusively to your company. They report to your controller or operations manager and follow your close calendar and daily workflows.
