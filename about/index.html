About Scalar Teams

Built by a CPA and supply-chain operator to hire the way operators actually work.

Scalar Teams places full-time finance, accounting, logistics, and senior AI engineering professionals from Latin America and Canada with US and Canadian companies. We hire and employ talent directly through our office in Ecuador and bill monthly in advance in USD.

Founder Story

25 years in wholesale distribution, cross-border perishables, and refrigerated freight

Scalar Teams was founded by Eduardo Polo, a CPA trained in Ecuador who has spent 25 years running physical operations across North and South America—including wholesale distribution, cross-border perishable supply chains, and refrigerated freight.

Based in British Columbia, Canada and bilingual in English and Spanish, Eduardo built Scalar Teams after decades of hiring and managing cross-border accounting, logistics, and technical teams for his own operations.

Most staffing firms are run by generalist recruiters who have never closed a general ledger, reconciled a freight subledger, or shipped production software. They forward keyword-matched résumés and leave the real vetting to the buyer.

At Scalar Teams, vetting starts from the actual workflows of the job:

  • For controllers and CFOs: testing candidates on US GAAP accruals, three-way PO matching, vendor bills, freight invoices, collections, QuickBooks, and NetSuite.
  • For CTOs and engineering leaders: verifying identity on live video and testing candidates on production LLM systems (RAG, agents, evaluation suites, and reliability)—not toy demos.
  • For every buyer: providing clear quotes and candidate evaluations in writing, without forcing you onto a sales call.

Our Ecuador Office & Employment Model

Direct employment as Employer of Record. One monthly USD invoice.

Cross-border hiring often stalls on legal entity setup, local labor compliance, or unreliable contractor arrangements. Our operational footprint removes that friction.

Employed through our Ecuador office

Talent is hired and employed through our office in Ecuador, where Scalar Teams acts as employer of record. We manage local employment contracts, payroll administration, and statutory obligations directly.

In-person identity & background checks

Because we operate on the ground in Ecuador and across our North and South American network, we verify government identification, educational credentials, and prior manager references directly.

Simple monthly billing in USD

Ecuador uses the US Dollar as its official currency and sits on Eastern Standard Time (UTC-5) year-round. We bill your company monthly in advance in USD on a single commercial invoice.

Canada & Latin America Talent Pool

Synchronous North American workdays across two talent corridors

Real-time collaboration requires shared working hours and clear communication. We recruit exclusively across Latin America and Canada so your hires work your schedule—whether your headquarters is on Pacific, Mountain, Central, or Eastern time.

Latin America (anchored in Ecuador)

Bilingual (English/Spanish) CPAs, senior and staff accountants, AP/AR specialists, freight coordinators, and senior software/AI engineers working full North American business hours with at least 6 hours of daily Pacific overlap.

Canada (anchored in British Columbia)

Senior AI/LLM engineers, machine learning engineers, and cross-border operations professionals familiar with US and Canadian accounting standards, enterprise software stacks, and North American business practices.
